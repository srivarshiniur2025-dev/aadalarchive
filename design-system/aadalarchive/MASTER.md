# Aadal Archive Design System — Master

> Source of truth for product UI. Page overrides live in `pages/`.
> Product: **Aadal Archive** — Where movement becomes memory.

## Brand statement

From inspiration to rehearsal to performance, every movement deserves a place.

Feel like: bright temple archive · silk & brass · classical dance moodboard · warm cultural stage.

Typography: Cormorant (display) · Great Vibes (script accents) · Cinzel (labels) · Outfit (UI).

Simple words. Cultural richness comes from color, photo, motif, and motion — not hard Sanskrit UI labels.
